How to play

  1. Roll once per day (resets at UTC midnight). The number is between 0 and 1,000,000.
  2. Your number is scanned for patterns: primes, palindromes, round numbers, sequences and more.
  3. Each matching badge earns Exclusivity Points (EP) — the rarer the pattern, the more EP.
  4. Share your result and chase streaks and a full badge collection.
